# Research basis

The task uses standard objects from rigorous computational dynamics and computational topology: closed outer approximations of nonlinear maps, Morse decompositions of directed multivalued maps, Conley index pairs, continuation through union systems, cubical homology over F2, generalized ranks and interval decompositions (barcodes) of zigzag representations, linear relations and their composition, and endorelation semigroups generated by closed continuation loops.

Kronecker invariants follow the classical Kronecker-Weierstrass classification of matrix pencils (equivalently, of finite-dimensional representations of the Kronecker quiver): a linear relation R in U + U, viewed through its two coordinate projections R -> U, decomposes uniquely into right singular blocks, left singular blocks, finite regular blocks indexed by powers of monic irreducible polynomials over F2, and infinite regular blocks. The graded record is the multiset of block types, which is a complete conjugacy invariant of the relation and does not depend on any choice of basis. No coordinate-dependent matrix normal form is graded.

Zigzag barcodes follow the interval decomposition theorem for type-A quiver representations (Gabriel; Carlsson and de Silva) and the inclusion-exclusion relation between interval multiplicities and generalized ranks (Kim and Memoli).

The instance generator is deterministic and fully contained in this bundle. No external data are required.
