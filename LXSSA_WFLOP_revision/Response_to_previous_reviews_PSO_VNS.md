## Summary of changes relative to the earlier answers

- **Proposed method changed:** LX-SSA-VNS became PSO-VNS. Once PSO was configured inside its convergence region, it outperformed SSA, LX-SSA and SSA-VNS. SSA-VNS was tied with a random-multistart control (RS-VNS), and the Laplace step gave no gain. This affects R1-4, R1-5, R2-3 and R3-1.
- **Not, or only partly, addressed in the new manuscript:**
  - R1-2: 5D/6D re-optimization of the proposed method (only SSA/LX-SSA were re-optimized).
  - R1-9: the full 80-turbine Horns Rev 1 farm (dropped; 16-turbine block plus IEA37 instead).
  - R2-3: φ/χ sensitivity (moot for the proposed method).
  - R2-5: the published Kusiak–Song comparison in the supplement (placeholder).
  - R3-4: verification of the new references.
  - R1-12: final proofreading of the version without placeholders.
- **New material not requested by the reviewers:** analysis of the PSO setting (Sec. VI-C), RS-VNS control, feasibility-preserving initialization, budget scaling to 120,030 calls (Sec. VII-A), IEA37 Case Study 1 (Sec. VII-C), budget-split study (Sec. VI-E), and public code/data.
