## Summary of changes relative to the earlier answers

- **Thesis changed:** the earlier paper proposed LX-SSA-VNS as a better algorithm. The new paper shows that (M1) the PSO baseline setting reverses the ranking of methods, (M2) the salp-swarm phases and the Laplace step add nothing beyond random sampling while a PSO phase does, and (M3) the simple two-phase PSO-VNS is the most consistent performer, with its limits stated. The controlled evaluation protocol and recommendations for benchmarking WFLOP methods are part of the contribution. This affects R1-4, R1-5, R1-7, R2-1, R2-3 and R3-1.
- **Not, or only partly, addressed in the new manuscript:**
  - R1-2: 5D/6D re-optimization of the proposed method (only SSA/LX-SSA were re-optimized).
  - R1-9: the full 80-turbine Horns Rev 1 farm (dropped; 16-turbine block plus IEA37 instead).
  - R2-3: φ/χ sensitivity (moot for the proposed method).
  - R2-5: the published Kusiak–Song comparison in the supplement (placeholder).
  - R3-4: verification of the new references.
  - R1-12: final proofreading of the version without placeholders.
- **New material not requested by the reviewers:** the baseline-setting analysis (*Effect of the Baseline Setting*, `tab:baseline`), RS-VNS control, feasibility-preserving initialization, budget scaling (*Feasibility-Preserving Initialization and Budget Scaling*), IEA37 Case Study 1, budget-split study, *Discussion and Recommendations* with a list of benchmarking recommendations, and public code/data.
