\section*{Data availability}
The supplied revision archive contains 11,640 per-run revision records, experiment drivers, generated tables and figure files. It does not include the 36,190 original optimization records or all core model, optimizer and analysis modules needed to reproduce the full study. The archive inventory and executable checks are described in the supplementary archive-audit section. The revision archive is available to the editor and reviewers from the corresponding author on request; completion and public deposition of a versioned reproducibility package remain pending, and no public repository is claimed in this manuscript. Horns Rev~1 and Lillgrund inputs derive from the PyWake package (Lillgrund wind climate: G\"o\c{c}men and Giebel, 2016), and IEA37 inputs from the IEA Wind Task~37 case-study repository.
