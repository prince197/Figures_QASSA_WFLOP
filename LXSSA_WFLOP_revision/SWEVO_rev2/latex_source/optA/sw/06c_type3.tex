\section{Wind Farm Type III: Lillgrund Block}
\label{sec:lillgrund}
\label{sec:farm-III}
\subsection{Experimental Setting}
The second site with a measured wind climate (Section~\ref{S-sec:S-xp-site}, Table~\ref{S-tab:S-xp-site}) is
\begin{itemize}
\item a $4\times4$ block of the Lillgrund offshore wind farm (Sweden; Siemens SWT-2.3-93, $D=93$~m),
\item with the 12-sector Weibull climate of PyWake's Lillgrund example~\cite{PyWake}, fitted to seven months of met-mast measurements at 61~m (hub height 65~m)~\cite{Gocmen2016},
\item the model and bins of Section~\ref{sec:hornsrev-site} (wake-free AEP 139.40~GWh/yr),
\item the parallelogram through the corner turbines as boundary, and
\item 6{,}030 and 30{,}030 evaluations, 30 seed-paired runs, random initialization.
\end{itemize}
The minimum spacing is $3D$, because 16 turbines do not fit into the block at $4D$ (installed minimum spacing: $3.29D$).

\subsection{Numerical Results}
PSO-VNS is feasible in 17/30 runs at 6,030 evaluations and 30/30 at 30,030, PSO in 0/30 and 19/30; GA, LX-SSA and DE reach no feasible layout at 6,030 evaluations (Table~\ref{tab:farm-III}). PSO-VNS ranks first at both budgets on its mean AEP (113.07 and 114.59~GWh/yr), but at 6,030 evaluations only MS-SLSQP is feasible in all 30 runs. No method reaches the installed block on average (116.10~GWh/yr; one PSO-VNS layout exceeds it by 0.05\%, also with 1$^\circ$ bins).

\subsection{Statistical Analysis}
The feasibility advantage of PSO-VNS over PSO is significant at both budgets (exact McNemar $p=1.5\times10^{-5}$ and $9.8\times10^{-4}$). In the seed-paired test, which scores feasibility first, MS-SLSQP is better than PSO-VNS at 6,030 evaluations ($p_{\rm Holm}=0.008$). At 30,030 evaluations PSO-VNS is better than GA (feasible in 21/30 runs), PSO, SSA-VNS, SSA, LX-SSA, DE and MS-SLSQP, but not significantly different from VNS ($p_{\rm Holm}=0.058$).

Re-evaluated with 1$^\circ$ bins (Table~\ref{S-tab:S-sa-lg-eval}), the installed block loses 1.65\% and the optimized layouts 1.14\% on average (PSO-VNS at 30,030: 1.69\%); at 30,030 evaluations PSO-VNS is no longer significantly better than SSA-VNS or MS-SLSQP, and at 6,030 MS-SLSQP has the higher mean AEP (112.10 vs.\ 111.97~GWh/yr). Under PyWake's NOJ model with 1$^\circ$ bins, MS-SLSQP has a higher mean AEP than PSO-VNS at both budgets (112.61 and 113.01~GWh/yr), and 16 optimized layouts exceed the installed block; the feasibility results do not depend on the evaluator.
