\section{Wind Farm Type III: Lillgrund Block}
\label{sec:lillgrund}
\label{sec:farm-III}
This section repeats the comparison of Section~\ref{sec:hornsrev-site} on a second site with a measured wind climate, a block of the Lillgrund offshore wind farm with a tighter spacing.

\subsection{Experimental Setting}
The wake model, the direction and speed bins, the penalty, the budgets, seeds and initialization, and the method settings are those of Type~II (Section~\ref{sec:hornsrev-site}). The settings that differ are (Table~\ref{tab:farm-types}; details: Section~\ref{S-sec:S-xp-site} and Table~\ref{S-tab:S-xp-site}):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Site and layout.} The $4\times4$ block at the eastern corner of the Lillgrund offshore wind farm (Sweden); its installed layout is the reference.
\item \emph{Turbines.} Siemens SWT-2.3-93 ($D=93$~m, hub height 65~m) with the power and thrust curves of PyWake's Lillgrund example~\cite{PyWake}.
\item \emph{Wind data.} The 12-sector Weibull climate of that example, fitted to seven months of met-mast measurements at 61~m~\cite{Gocmen2016}; wake-free AEP 139.40~GWh/yr.
\item \emph{Constraints.} Boundary: the parallelogram through the corner turbines (enlarged by 0.2\%). Minimum spacing $\ell_{\min}=3D$, because 16 turbines do not fit into the block at $4D$ (installed minimum spacing: $3.29D$).
\item \emph{Methods.} The nine methods, GA included, at both budgets; the search box and the $r$ of the VNS radii follow the rule of Type~II (half-width: the largest absolute coordinate of the parallelogram). The control RSD-VNS, which here draws its Phase-1 samples in the parallelogram, was also run (Table~\ref{S-tab:S-xp-site}).
\end{enumerate}

PSO-VNS has the highest mean AEP at both budgets (Table~\ref{tab:farm-III}), but at 6,030 evaluations it is feasible in only 17 of 30 runs, and only MS-SLSQP is feasible in all 30. No method reaches the installed block on average; one PSO-VNS layout exceeds it by 0.05\%, also with 1$^\circ$ bins.

\subsection{Statistical Analysis}
In the seed-paired tests of Table~\ref{tab:farm-III}, which score feasibility first, MS-SLSQP is better than PSO-VNS at 6,030 evaluations, and at 30,030 evaluations PSO-VNS is better than every other method except VNS. The feasibility advantage of PSO-VNS over PSO is significant at both budgets, but with the final layouts re-evaluated with 1$^\circ$ bins, its lead over the runners-up SSA-VNS, VNS and MS-SLSQP at 30,030 evaluations is not significant, and MS-SLSQP has the higher mean AEP with 1$^\circ$ bins at 6,030 evaluations and under PyWake's NOJ model at both budgets (Table~\ref{tab:farm-III-tests}).
