\begin{letter}{Editor\\Journal of Modern Power Systems and Clean Energy}
\opening{Dear Editor,}

Please find the revised manuscript entitled \textit{``Application of the Laplacian Salp Swarm Algorithm to Continuous Wind Farm Layout Optimization.''}

We have substantially revised the manuscript in response to the reviewers' technical concerns. A central clarification is that the Laplacian Salp Swarm Algorithm (LX-SSA) is \emph{not proposed or designed in this manuscript}; it was previously introduced by Solanki and Deep in 2023. The present study evaluates the application of that existing optimizer to the continuous wind farm layout optimization problem (WFLOP).

The revision strengthens the physical and algorithmic description, including an explicit derivation of the wake-induced Weibull scale transformation, clearer justification and limitations of the Jensen wake model and 4D benchmark spacing, a consistent statement of the legacy power-curve model, explicit constraint-handling rules, corrected leader/follower pseudocode, and a complete WFLOP application workflow. The literature review has also been updated to include gradient/pseudo-gradient, Gaussian-wake, machine-learning, CFD-surrogate, and data--physics hybrid WFLO approaches. Strong performance claims have been moderated, and the manuscript now separates direct algorithmic evidence from historical literature benchmarks.

We have not created or inferred missing experimental evidence. Where the supplied archive does not contain raw run-level data, standardized runtime logs, exact coordinate tables, or site-measured wind data, the revised manuscript states those limitations rather than reporting unsupported statistical or practical claims.

Thank you for considering the revised work.

\closing{Sincerely,\\Prince Solanki\\on behalf of the authors}
