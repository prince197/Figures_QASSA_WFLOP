Table~\ref{tab:iea37} and Fig.~\ref{fig:layouts} place our methods in the context of IEA37 Case Study~1, in which the participants chose their own optimizers and budgets~\cite{Baker2019}. The best feasible submitted layouts reach 418,924.4~MWh for 16 turbines and 863,676.3~MWh for 36 turbines. At 30,030 calls, the best PSO-VNS layout would rank \NIEARankSixteenThirtyK{} among the \NIEAPubFeasSixteen{} feasible submitted 16-turbine layouts and \NIEARankThirtySixThirtyK{} among the \NIEAPubFeasThirtySix{} feasible 36-turbine layouts; it differs from the best feasible published layout by \NIEAGapSixteenThirtyK\% and \NIEAGapThirtySixThirtyK\% (\NIEAGapSixteenSixK\% and \NIEAGapThirtySixSixK\% at 6,030 calls), and the mean over the 30 runs by \NIEAMeanGapSixteenThirtyK\% and \NIEAMeanGapThirtySixThirtyK\%. Since the participants' optimizers and budgets differ and are not reported in a comparable form, this comparison is indicative only.
