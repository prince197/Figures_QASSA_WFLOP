\begin{abstract}
Wind farm layouts fix a plant's lifetime energy yield, yet comparisons of layout optimizers are hard to trust. This paper shows that, in continuous wind farm layout optimization, their conclusions depend strongly on baseline configuration and comparison control. Under a controlled protocol (68 benchmark cases, 6,030 evaluations including gradient evaluations, seed-paired runs, feasibility-aware ranking, Holm-corrected tests), a convergent particle swarm optimization (PSO) followed by basic variable neighborhood search (PSO-VNS) is the most consistent of eight methods, while salp-swarm phases add little beyond random sampling and the Laplace step makes the hybrid worse. First, the PSO setting $w=0.7$, $c_1=c_2=2$ violates the order-2 stability condition; with it, PSO is feasible in \NOldPSOFeas\% of the runs and ranks behind salp-swarm hybrids, whereas with the constriction setting it ranks first. Second, against a random-sampling control, a PSO phase gives VNS a clearly better start than salp-swarm phases, and the VNS phase significantly improves only the salp-swarm methods. Third, PSO-VNS has the best average rank (\NRankPSOVNS), is feasible in every run, and has the highest mean yield on the Horns Rev~1 block at 6,030 and 30,030 evaluations; however, it is not significantly different from PSO overall ($p=\NPWPSO$), \NFbFeasBestRank{} ranks first from feasible starts and \NBestGauss{} under a Gaussian re-evaluation of the final layouts, and a gap to the best published IEA Wind Task~37 layouts remains.

\section*{Nomenclature}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Nomenclature}
\begin{IEEEdescription}[\IEEEusemathlabelsep\IEEEsetlabelwidth{$w$, $c_1$, $c_2$}]
\item[$B$] Budget (number of evaluations).
\item[$C_T$, $K$] Thrust coefficient; Jensen wake-spreading constant.
\item[$D$, $R$] Rotor diameter and rotor radius ($D=2R$).
\item[$E_{\rm ideal}$] Wake-free benchmark objective of one turbine.
\item[$F_p$] Penalized wake loss (minimized).
\item[$\ell_{\min}$] Minimum spacing between turbines.
\item[$N$, $N_p$] Number of turbines; population size.
\item[$N_\theta$, $N_s$] Numbers of wind-direction and wind-speed bins.
\item[$r$] Farm radius.
\item[$w$, $c_1$, $c_2$] Inertia weight and acceleration coefficients of PSO.
\item[$\mathbf X$] Layout vector $(x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_N,y_N)$.
\item[$\theta$] Wind direction (blowing towards, CCW from east).
\item[$\omega$] Fraction of the budget given to Phase~1.
\end{IEEEdescription}
