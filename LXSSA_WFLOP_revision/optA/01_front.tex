\begin{abstract}
Wind farm layouts fix a plant's lifetime energy yield, yet comparisons of layout optimizers are hard to trust. We compare eight methods for continuous wind farm layout optimization under a controlled protocol (68 benchmark cases, 6,030 evaluations, feasibility-aware ranking, Holm-corrected and equivalence tests). First, baseline configuration changes the conclusions: the particle swarm optimization (PSO) setting $w=0.7$, $c_1=c_2=2$ violates the order-2 stability condition, so the swarm does not contract and most evaluations are infeasible; with it, PSO ranks behind salp-swarm hybrids; with the constriction setting it ranks first. Second, as the first phase before variable neighborhood search (VNS), a salp swarm (SSA) adds nothing beyond random sampling in the farm, a Laplacian SSA (LX-SSA) is worse (the Laplace step is harmful), and PSO gives a clear gain; at this budget, VNS acts as a local-search descent. Third, PSO-VNS (PSO followed by VNS) and a well-configured PSO are practically equivalent on average (within $\pm\NEqMargin$ percentage points of wake loss; posterior probability above 0.99); PSO-VNS adds feasibility reliability (Horns Rev~1 block: \NHRFeasPSOVNS{} vs \NHRFeasPSO{} feasible runs) and gains in larger Data Set~II layouts. These conclusions hold across qualification thresholds, farm clusters and one Holm family. PSO-VNS is a consistent reference method, not a superior one: VNS alone ranks first from feasible starts, and the best published IEA Wind Task~37 layouts remain better.

\section*{Nomenclature}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Nomenclature}
\begin{IEEEdescription}[\IEEEusemathlabelsep\IEEEsetlabelwidth{$w$, $c_1$, $c_2$}]
\item[$B$] Budget (number of evaluations).
\item[$C_T$, $K$] Thrust coefficient; Jensen wake-spreading constant.
\item[$D$, $R$] Rotor diameter and rotor radius ($D=2R$).
\item[$E_{\rm ideal}$] Wake-free benchmark objective of one turbine.
\item[$F_p$] Penalized wake loss (minimized).
\item[$\ell_{\min}$] Minimum spacing between turbines.
\item[$N$, $N_p$] Number of turbines; population size.
\item[$N_\theta$, $N_s$] Numbers of wind-direction and wind-speed bins.
\item[$r$] Farm radius.
\item[$w$, $c_1$, $c_2$] Inertia weight and acceleration coefficients of PSO.
\item[$\mathbf X$] Layout vector $(x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_N,y_N)$.
\item[$\theta$] Wind direction (blowing towards, CCW from east).
\item[$\omega$] Fraction of the budget given to Phase~1.
\end{IEEEdescription}
