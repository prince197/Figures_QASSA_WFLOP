\section*{Data Availability}
The following are openly available at \TBD{Zenodo DOI}: 1) the source code of all methods and of the benchmark evaluator; 2) the per-run results of all studies, i.e., for every run the method, case, seed, budget, initialization, number of evaluations, final objective, feasibility, final turbine coordinates and convergence curve, including the runs with the earlier PSO setting; and 3) the scripts that implement the evaluation protocol (seed-paired runs, feasibility-aware ranking, case-mean and run-level tests with Holm correction, and the re-evaluation with the alternative power curve and wake model). One command (\texttt{sh analysis/build\_mpce\_paper.sh}) regenerates every table, figure and number of this paper and of its supplementary material from the per-run results. The supplementary material contains the model details, the pseudocode of SSA and LX-SSA, the wind data, the per-case results of all studies, the coordinates of the best layouts and further figures.
